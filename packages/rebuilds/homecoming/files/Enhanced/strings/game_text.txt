[IDS_ENH_TIPS_1_TEXT]Hold LT (Shift) to enter combat stance and face the nearest enemy. Your attacks, dodge and shove only work in stance; outside it the same buttons run, roll and open the map.
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_2_TEXT]In stance, RB (left mouse) is a quick light attack and RT (right mouse) a slow heavy attack. Hold the heavy attack to charge it. A few light attacks finished with a heavy one make a combo.
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_3_TEXT]Hold LB (Q) to guard. Blocked hits do a quarter of their damage and cost stamina, and an attacker whose hits you block will falter, giving you an opening.
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_4_TEXT]Press block just before a hit lands to parry it: you take no damage and the attacker staggers, open to a heavy attack.
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_5_TEXT]Grabs, knockdowns and attacks from behind can't be blocked. In stance, press B (Space) with a direction to side-step them. Outside stance, B (a tap of Space) rolls, to cover ground or get clear. Both cost stamina.
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_6_TEXT]Blocking hits, dodging, rolling and running tire Alex out; the bar under the screen shows how much he has left. When it runs out he can't run or dodge and his guard breaks, until he gets his breath back.
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_7_TEXT]In stance, Y (E) shoves the closest enemy in front of you back a step. It does no damage, but buys you room to escape or set up an attack.
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_8_TEXT]Light attacks won't stun. Heavy attacks, parries and counters will, and a stunned enemy can be finished with a heavy attack.
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_9_TEXT]Enemies are drawn to your flashlight; D-pad up (F) turns it off and on. Outside combat stance, RT (hold Space) runs.
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_10_TEXT]D-pad down (X) drinks a health drink, or uses a first aid kit if you have none. Everything you carry is in the inventory: Back (I).
[IDS_ENH_TIPS_11_TEXT]Save at the red seals. RB (Tab) outside combat stance opens the map, where the objectives are listed too.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSCREATURES_1_TEXT]Once a Swarm latches on your health drains; press dodge to shake them off. They often hover in front of you before attacking: a perfect moment to strike.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSCREATURES_2_TEXT]A Feral hops before a turn when it is about to lunge: dodge aside and counter for an easy stun. Shooting one mid-charge drops it, and quick weapons like the knife work well.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSCREATURES_3_TEXT]Needlers block frontal attacks, so get to their side first. When one retreats to the ceiling, be ready to dodge. A headshot is the quickest and safest way to stop one.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSCREATURES_4_TEXT]The Lurker's head is weak to heavy attacks. Be careful around water: you never know what is beneath it.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSCREATURES_5_TEXT]Schism rears back before charging in to grab you: dodge when you see it. Its attacks can knock you down, so keep your distance.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSCREATURES_6_TEXT]Soldiers with pipes dodge your attacks. Keep your guard up and time your attacks carefully.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSCREATURES_7_TEXT]When you are knocked down, tap dodge at the right moment to get back on your feet: watch for the cloud of dust.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSBOSSES_1_TEXT]Wait for Smog to expand his lungs: exposed, they are his weak point. His smoke chokes you and hurts, so stay out of it.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSBOSSES_2_TEXT]Siam's female side is weaker, and attacks from behind do much more damage. After a knockdown Siam tries to pummel you, so get up quickly.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSBOSSES_3_TEXT]Destroy the suspended flesh sacks to bring Sepulcher down. When he attacks from the ground, strike his arms to expose his body: lock on with R3 (middle mouse) and flick the right stick to aim at either arm.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSBOSSES_4_TEXT]Breaking Scarlet's porcelain armour stuns her; start with her legs or chest. Shatter all of it before you can pry off her back piece.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSBOSSES_5_TEXT]Approach Amnion from the side to get inside her attacks; hitting her legs eventually brings her down. When she showers acid, get underneath her.
[IDS_ENH_TIPSBOSSES_6_TEXT]Dodging Asphyxia's grab leaves her tail exposed. Striking the tail brings her to her knees, leaving her head open.
